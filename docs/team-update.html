CampusGuard: final update for the team

The project is complete and tested. The report and slides are drafted from the final results, and we submit tomorrow. Please read the numbers below before editing anything, because several have changed.

The one thing everyone must get right

The student dataset is simulated. No usable public dataset of real student logins exists: the closest, HIED-AuthLog, is embargoed until 23 December 2026. Never call the data "real", and never present these results as real-world accuracy. Do not use the old 99.98% figure as a result.

What we can say

  • In simulation, it stopped all remote attackers while rarely interrupting students.
  • Rules and OTP do most of the work.
  • The Isolation Forest adds little: 39.5% to 38.2%, within the margin of error, and no change in an independent run.
  • Attackers who look like the student mostly get in, and we measured that.

What we must not say

  • "Trained on real student data."
  • "99.98% accurate." That score came from easy data and fell to 0.835 on realistic decisions.
  • "The ML model detects attacks." It is one supplementary signal.
  • "This would stop 62% of attacks in production." That figure depends on the mix of attacks we chose.
